Good to know

About Swarr

A few answers before you press play.

Is Swarr really free?

Yes. Streaming and downloads are free with no ads and no subscription. Swarr is funded by merch, live ticket commissions and optional tips, so the music itself never sits behind a paywall.

Do downloads expire?

No. A downloaded file is a normal audio file in the folder you chose. Nothing re-checks it, nothing locks it, and it plays on other apps and players too.

Do I need an account?

No. You can stream, download and keep a full library without signing in. Everything lives right on your Android device.

What audio quality can I get?

Streaming defaults to 256 kbps AAC with a lossless FLAC tier, and downloads go up to 320 kbps. Local files are played bit-perfect, including FLAC, WAV and Opus.

Which devices are supported?

Swarr is built exclusively for Android — supporting Android 8.0 and higher across phones and tablets.

How are artists paid?

Artists keep 70% of optional tips and 100% of direct support. Streaming pays out from a pool split by actual listening time rather than by track count.
